## **Founder Story**

Abu didn’t choose the mountain. The mountain chose him.

Born in Lokoro, a small village on Kilimanjaro’s slopes, he grew up where most kids see their future mapped out by geography. His father worked the mountain—a porter, a guide—and Abu spent his childhood watching climbers from distant countries arrive with expensive gear and wild dreams. He’d stare up at the glaciers above his village and wonder: What would it feel like to stand there?

At 18, Abu didn’t have the luxury of wondering much longer. He started as a porter. The hardest job on the mountain. Carrying loads up 5,895 meters while older guides barked orders. The work was brutal. But it was his way in.

He learned something most guides never do: what it feels like to be at the bottom. He carried loads through storms, fell behind the group, nearly quit. He watched porters get treated like they didn’t matter. That stuck with him.

Over the next few years, Abu moved through every role. Camp waiter, camp manager, assistant guide. Each step wasn’t a promotion; it was a lesson. By the time he became a full guide, he didn’t just know the mountain’s routes. He knew every person who worked them and what they needed to keep going.

When Abu founded Kilimanjaro True Venture, it wasn’t to get rich. He had a simple vision: create a company where people actually matter. Where the person carrying your pack gets paid fairly and respected. Where your guide remembers your name and knows you’re scared, but won’t let you quit.

Today, Abu brings you not just to the summit. He brings you to meet the real Tanzania. To understand why this mountain, these people, and these journeys matter. He’s still here. Still climbing. Still leading every season.

# Charity Climbs & Community Impact

At Kilimanjaro True Venture, adventure is not only about reaching the summit—it is also about creating a positive impact in the communities that make these journeys possible. Through our community initiative, Abu Hope Foundation, we aim to connect travel with meaningful change and social responsibility.

Founded in 2019 by Abdallah Athumani (Abu), Abu Hope Foundation works to support local communities in Tanzania through initiatives focused on health, education, and environmental awareness. The foundation was created from a deep desire to give back to the communities surrounding the mountains and national parks that are at the heart of Tanzania’s tourism industry.

Many of the people who live near Mount Kilimanjaro and the tourism regions of northern Tanzania face challenges in accessing basic resources such as education opportunities, health services, and environmental support. Through Abu Hope Foundation, we aim to contribute to small but meaningful improvements that help empower communities and create long-term positive change.

Guests traveling with Kilimanjaro True Venture are welcome to become part of these efforts. Visitors who are interested in learning more about the foundation or participating in community activities can inform us in advance, and we will gladly organize opportunities for them to meet the local community and see the work of the foundation firsthand.

In addition, we encourage what we call Adventure for Charity—special climbing experiences where travelers can combine their journey with a purpose by supporting community initiatives through fundraising or direct involvement. These charity climbs allow guests not only to experience the challenge and beauty of climbing Mount Kilimanjaro but also to contribute to projects that help local communities thrive.

Through Abu Hope Foundation, our mission is simple but powerful: to empower communities and inspire hope. We believe that tourism can be a force for good, bringing people from around the world together while creating opportunities that benefit the communities that make these adventures possible.

# **3 Day Safari – Tarangire, Ngorongoro & Lake Manyara**

Starting from $1,365 per person

This carefully designed 3-day safari offers an incredible introduction to the diverse beauty of northern Tanzania. Within a short time, travelers experience three completely different ecosystems—each with its own unique landscapes, wildlife, and atmosphere.

From the ancient baobab trees and large elephant herds of Tarangire National Park, to the world-famous wildlife haven of Ngorongoro Crater, and the lush green forests and scenic lake views of Lake Manyara National Park, this safari is rich in both variety and unforgettable moments.

---

## **Detailed Itinerary**

### Day 1: Arusha to Tarangire National Park – Land of Giants

Your safari begins with a morning departure from Arusha, heading toward Tarangire National Park, a park known for its untouched beauty and large concentrations of wildlife.

As you enter the park, you are welcomed by iconic baobab trees, often referred to as the “Tree of Life,” standing tall across the landscape. Tarangire is especially famous for its large elephant herds, where you may witness families interacting, feeding, and moving gracefully across the plains.

The Tarangire River, which flows through the park, acts as a lifeline for wildlife, attracting animals such as giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, buffalo, and predators like lions and leopards. During your game drive, your guide will take you to key wildlife areas, explaining animal behavior and the ecosystem.

You will enjoy a picnic lunch inside the park, surrounded by nature, before continuing your afternoon game drive. In the evening, transfer to your lodge for dinner and relaxation.

---

### Day 2: Ngorongoro Crater – Africa’s Natural Wonder

After an early breakfast, you will travel to the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater, one of the most unique wildlife destinations in the world.

The crater is a massive volcanic caldera that forms a natural enclosure for wildlife, creating one of the highest concentrations of animals in Africa. This is one of the best places to spot the Big Five, including the rare black rhino.

As you explore the crater floor, you will encounter a variety of habitats including grasslands, swamps, forests, and a soda lake. Each area supports different types of wildlife, from hippos resting in water pools to lions resting in the open plains.

Your guide will provide detailed insights into the geology, wildlife, and history of the crater, making this experience both educational and exciting. After a full day of exploration, you will ascend the crater and return to your lodge.

---

### **Day 3:** Lake Manyara National Park – Forest, Lake & Wildlife

On your final day, you will visit Lake Manyara National Park, a scenic park known for its diversity and beauty.

Lake Manyara is famous for its lush groundwater forest, where monkeys, birds, and elephants can be seen moving through the trees. The park is also known for its tree-climbing lions, a rare and fascinating behavior.

As you drive through the park, you will experience a variety of environments—from dense forest to open plains and the shores of the lake, often filled with flamingos and other bird species.

After the game drive, you will return to Arusha, marking the end of a short but incredibly rich safari experience.
